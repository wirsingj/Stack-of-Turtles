# Physics Notes

These notes define the guardrails for the first version of Stack of Turtles.

## Framing

The app is a bottom-up conceptual map. It should answer "what can stable structure become?" rather than "what is this made of if we cut it smaller?"

The core sequence is:

```text
fields -> particles -> hadrons -> nuclei -> atoms -> molecules -> polymers -> cells -> organisms -> minds/culture
```

## Accuracy Rules

Protons define the element because proton count sets nuclear charge.

Neutrons define isotopes and strongly affect nuclear stability, but they do not by themselves change the element.

Electrons are elementary in the Standard Model. Their configurations around nuclei drive most ordinary chemistry.

Protons and neutrons are hadrons, specifically baryons. A proton has valence content `uud`; a neutron has valence content `udd`.

Quarks are not shown as free ordinary objects because color confinement matters.

Most proton and neutron mass is not the rest mass of the valence quarks. It comes from QCD field energy, gluons, and quark dynamics.

Atoms are not little solar systems. Orbital visuals should be labeled as conceptual electron-state imagery, not literal paths.

Molecules depend on electron density, quantum states, geometry, polarity, and energy. The app can simplify bonds visually, but should not imply that bonds are rigid sticks.

Life should be framed as organized chemistry over time: boundary, metabolism, information, repair, and feedback.

Mind/culture should be framed carefully. The app can say physical systems can model, symbolize, and coordinate; it should not claim to solve consciousness.

## Useful Copy Patterns

Use "becomes possible" instead of "is merely."

Use "stable pattern" when moving from lower layers to higher layers.

Use "description boundary" when reaching the bottom of the app's scope.

Use "conceptual visual" when the drawing is explanatory rather than physically literal.

## Anti-Patterns

Do not say everything is "just atoms" or "just particles."

Do not imply that emergence violates physics.

Do not imply that Standard Model particles explain every higher-level phenomenon by themselves.

Do not use a top-down microscope metaphor as the main navigation.
